Authorized testing only

Every technique in these labs is taught for defensive education and authorized assessment, and runs inside an isolated practice range. Using these tools against systems you do not own or have written permission to test is illegal in most jurisdictions.

How the labs map to your modules

The lab series follows the EC-Council Ethical Hacking lab track (CEH v10-era numbering). Your course is organized into 20 CEH v13 modules. The two overlap heavily but aren't numbered the same, so each card below shows the v13 module it supports. Where a tool has aged since the lab was written, the lab's own page notes the current equivalent.
